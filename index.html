Samsung Galaxy S8 / S8+ Complete Repair Manual

Tools Required: Digital heat gun, metal opening blade, plastic cards, T7000 adhesive, Phillips #00.

1. Curved OLED Screen & Frame Assembly Swap

  1. Power off phone and remove SIM/MicroSD tray.
  2. Apply heat to curved back glass panel for 3 minutes. Insert razor blade at bottom seam and slice perimeter adhesive.
  3. Lift back glass carefully (note: fingerprint sensor flex cable is attached to back glass; disconnect immediately before pulling away).
  4. Unscrew upper motherboard cover and loudspeaker box. Disconnect battery connector.
  5. Transfer motherboard, cameras, and battery into new replacement frame assembly. Seal back glass with T7000 adhesive.

Samsung Galaxy S9 / S9+ Complete Repair Manual

Tools Required: Heat gun, razor cards, Phillips #00, B7000 adhesive.

1. Curved Display & Back Glass Replacement

  1. Power off and remove SIM tray. Heat back glass perimeter.
